# Visual Effects

## Rule: Gradients, glow, and glassmorphism are allowed with constraints

Use gradients, glow, and glassmorphism only when they strengthen technology feeling without reducing data readability.

Why:
新涨乐 can feel technological, but market and trading information must remain readable and trustworthy.

Verify:

- Effects do not cover or reduce contrast of key prices, change values, volume, or actions.
- Effects do not use red/green unless communicating financial state.
- Effect colors come from approved tokens or derived tokens recorded in `assets/tokens/token-mapping.json`.
- Effects remain secondary to the data and controls.

## Rule: Do not replace hierarchy with effects

Do not use glow, glass, or gradients as the main way to explain page structure.

Why:
Financial tools need stable hierarchy from layout, typography, spacing, and surfaces.

Verify:

- Page structure remains clear if decorative effects are removed.
- Cards, lists, tabs, and buttons still follow their component rules.
